are about 3.251 km apart edge-to-edge. These are computed map distances, not

Transfer the existing landscape and native morphology methods without changing
scales, weights or feature thresholds. New paths prevent overwriting the frozen
Cave Ridge experiment. The new area overlaps that study's southwest edge, and
Cave Ridge references have already been seen: this is unvalidated prospecting,
not a new independent blind validation. No Denny cave inventories, entrance
records, photographs or exploration reports will be consulted.

Acquire fresh whitelisted DNR geological layers and USFS trail/ownership context;
reuse the checksummed public USGS 1 m DEM tiles. Evaluate finer geological
coverage without generic point layers. Keep geological/model priority separate
from access/terrain screening. Do not route directly to cliffs or assume that
a point on public mapped land is safely/legal reachable. No cave entry, mine
entry, excavation, or disturbance is proposed. No outreach is authorized.

Deliver standard GeoTIFFs/GeoPackages, ranked surface objects, per-object access
flags, a persistent QGIS project free of known-entrance layers, map previews,
provenance/hashes, verification and a concise interpretation of practical limits.
